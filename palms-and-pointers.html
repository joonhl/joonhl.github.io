ch.01 · roots

Valley Heat

Temecula, CA · c. 1998 – 2010

Inland heat, vineyard rows past the window, and hot-air balloons hanging over the ridge before the sun cleared it. A Tamagotchi came apart on the garage floor one afternoon - not broken, just being understood. Curiosity ran the household long before Wi-Fi did.
